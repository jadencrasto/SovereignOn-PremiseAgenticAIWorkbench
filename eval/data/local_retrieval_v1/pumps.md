# Synthetic pump handbook
## P-101
### Operating envelope
#### Normal
Normal discharge pressure is 4 bar.

#### Warning
Warning discharge pressure is 5 bar.

#### Alarm
Alarm discharge pressure is 6 bar.

### Inspection procedure
Inspect the seal for leakage every 12 hours.
Record the seal inspection in the shift log.

## P-102
