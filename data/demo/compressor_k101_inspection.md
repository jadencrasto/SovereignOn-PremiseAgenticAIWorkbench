# EQUIPMENT INSPECTION & OVERHAUL REPORT: K-101 WET GAS COMPRESSOR
**Document ID:** DOC-INSP-2026-K101
**Date of Inspection:** 2026-02-14
**Equipment Tag:** K-101 (Wet Gas Compressor Train A)
**Unit:** Fluid Catalytic Cracking Unit (FCCU) - Gas Recovery Section
**Plant Location:** On-Premise Synthetic Refinery Complex
**Inspector:** S. K. Verma (Senior Reliability Engineer)

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Wet Gas Compressor K-101 underwent scheduled major overhaul and diagnostic vibration analysis following repeated high vibration alarms on the driven-end (DE) bearing housing. Total running hours since last major overhaul: 34,200 hours. The primary root causes identified were dynamic rotor unbalance due to hydrocarbon polymer deposition on the Stage 3 impeller and severe clearance degradation of the carbon ring seal assembly.

## 2. KEY OBSERVATIONS & MEASUREMENTS
- **Vibration Amplitude (Pre-Overhaul):**
  - Driven-End Bearing (Horizontal): 9.4 mm/s RMS (Alarm threshold: 7.1 mm/s RMS, Trip threshold: 11.2 mm/s RMS).
  - Predominant frequency component: 1X rotational frequency (4,980 RPM), confirming mechanical unbalance.
  - Non-Driven-End Bearing (Vertical): 4.8 mm/s RMS (Acceptable range).
- **Rotor & Impeller Condition:**
  - Stage 1 and Stage 2 impellers exhibited moderate surface fouling (0.4 mm sticky hydrocarbon residue).
  - Stage 3 impeller showed heavy, non-uniform polymer buildup on suction vanes, causing 42 grams unbalance.
  - Rotor runout measured at shaft center: 0.038 mm (within allowable tolerance of 0.050 mm).
- **Bearing & Seal Clearances:**
  - DE Journal Bearing clearance: 0.165 mm (Design specification: 0.100 - 0.130 mm; exceeded tolerance by 27%).
  - NDE Journal Bearing clearance: 0.120 mm (Within design specification).
  - Thrust bearing pad wear: 0.08 mm reduction in babbitt lining on active pads.
  - Carbon Ring Dry Gas Seal (DE): Inboard seal faces scored with micro-cracking; nitrogen barrier seal buffer gas leakage increased from 4.2 Nm³/h to 14.8 Nm³/h.

## 3. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS COMPLETED
1. Complete mechanical chemical cleaning of rotor assembly using non-chlorinated solvent wash.
2. Dynamic balance of rotor in high-speed balancing facility to ISO 1940-1 Grade G1.0 (< 1.2 g-mm/kg residual unbalance).
3. Replacement of DE and NDE tilt-pad journal bearings with new OEM babbitt-lined bearings; final assembled clearance verified at 0.112 mm (DE) and 0.110 mm (NDE).
4. Full replacement of DE tandem dry gas seal cartridge (Model DGS-400-SS) and calibration of nitrogen buffer differential pressure controller (set point maintained at +0.75 bar above sealing cavity).
5. Lubrication system flush: Replaced 1,200 liters of ISO VG 46 turbine oil; oil sample particle count returned ISO 4406 cleanliness level 15/13/10.

## 4. POST-MAINTENANCE ACCEPTANCE TESTING
Post-commissioning full-load test run conducted for 48 hours:
- DE Bearing vibration: **2.1 mm/s RMS** (Good / Zone A).
- DE Bearing metal temperature: **64.2°C** (Normal operating range: 60°C - 75°C, trip limit: 95°C).
- Dry gas seal nitrogen leakage: **3.8 Nm³/h** (Design baseline).

## 5. RECOMMENDATIONS FOR RELIABILITY
1. Inspect suction knock-out drum V-101 mist eliminator pads during next turnaround to prevent polymer carryover.
2. Maintain weekly lube oil moisture sampling; alarm threshold set at 150 ppm H2O.
3. Next planned major inspection scheduled for February 2028.
