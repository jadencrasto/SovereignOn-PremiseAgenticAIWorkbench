# PIPING CORROSION MONITORING SURVEY: ATMOSPHERIC OVERHEAD SYSTEM
**Document ID:** DOC-CORR-2026-PL12
**Survey Date:** 2026-01-10
**Piping Circuit Tag:** 16"-CDU-OVHD-PL-12 (Crude Distillation Column C-101 Overhead Vapor Line)
**Material of Construction:** Carbon Steel ASTM A106 Grade B (Nominal Schedule: 40, Nominal Wall: 9.53 mm)
**Inspection Standard:** API 570 Piping Inspection Code
**NDT Method:** Ultrasonic Thickness Gauging (UT) & Pulsed Eddy Current (PEC)

---

## 1. SURVEY OBJECTIVE & SCOPE
Routine thickness measurement inspection of 48 Condition Monitoring Locations (CMLs) along the 16-inch overhead vapor line from Column C-101 top to Air Cooler E-101 inlet. This circuit handles wet hydrocarbon vapor, steam, and trace hydrogen chloride ($HCl$), making it susceptible to aqueous chloride and ammonium bisulfide corrosion.

## 2. MEASUREMENT RESULTS & CORROSION SUMMARY
- **Overall Corrosion Rate:** Average circuit corrosion rate determined at **0.18 mm/year** (Long-term rate over 5 years: 0.14 mm/year).
- **Critical Locations (CML-14 & CML-15 - Downstream of Neutralizing Amine Injection Quill):**
  - **CML-14 (90° Elbow Extrados):**
    - Nominal Wall Thickness: 9.53 mm
    - Previous Reading (2024): 5.10 mm
    - Current Reading (2026): **4.22 mm**
    - Minimum Required Thickness ($t_{\text{min}}$ per ASME B31.3): **3.80 mm**
    - Remaining Corrosion Allowance: **0.42 mm**
    - Estimated Remaining Life: **2.3 years** at current thinning rate (0.44 mm/year local rate).
  - **CML-15 (Top of Horizontal Run):**
    - Current Reading: **4.85 mm** ($t_{\text{min}}$: 3.80 mm).
- **Root Cause of Accelerated Local Thinning:**
  - Neutralizing amine injection quill Q-101 atomizing nozzle was partially eroded, resulting in poor dispersion and local water-washing acid condensation directly impacting the elbow extrados.

## 3. IMMEDIATE MITIGATION & RECOMMENDATIONS
1. **Operating Setpoints:** Increase water wash injection rate from 18 m³/h to 24 m³/h to ensure complete salt dissolution prior to the condensing zone.
2. **Quill Replacement:** Scheduled replacement of injection quill Q-101 with Hastelloy C-276 anti-vortex quill during planned minor outage in May 2026.
3. **Inspection Frequency:** Flag CML-14 and CML-15 for high-priority UT thickness re-measurement every 90 days.
4. **Piping Replacement Planning:** Prepare isometric fabrication drawings for replacing 12 meters of 16-inch piping from CML-12 to CML-18 with 316L clad piping during next major refinery turnaround (2027).
