## 1. INCIDENT DESCRIPTION & WORK SCOPE
Boiler Feed Water Pump P-204 was taken offline following high temperature alarms on the DE radial bearing (recorded peak temperature: 88.4°C, Alarm limit: 80°C, Trip limit: 90°C) combined with audible high-frequency cavitation noise and intermittent discharge pressure drops from 68 bar to 54 bar.

## 2. ROOT CAUSE INVESTIGATION
Disassembly and precision dimensional inspection revealed three distinct failure mechanisms:
1. **Cavitation & Flow Starvation:**
   - Suction strainer S-204 was found 65% clogged with magnetite scale and iron oxide particulates, causing Net Positive Suction Head Available (NPSHa) to drop below required NPSHr (3.2 m available vs 4.8 m required).
   - Impeller Stage 1 suction eye exhibited severe honeycomb pitting erosion across 40% of the blade inlet edges.
2. **Bearing Overheating & Lubricant Degradation:**
   - DE cylindrical roller bearing (SKF NU 318) inner ring raceway showed severe micro-spalling and brown lacquer discoloration due to thermal oxidation of grease lubricant.
   - Oil flinger ring on NDE bearing had detached from shaft locking screw, starving outer bearing cavity of oil mist.
3. **Mechanical Seal Flush Failure:**
   - API Plan 23 heat exchanger cooling water jacket had scale buildup, causing seal chamber temperature to rise to 118°C (recommended max: 80°C), resulting in thermal distortion of silicon carbide seal faces.

## 3. PARTS REPLACED & REPAIRS EXECUTED
- **Impeller:** Replaced Stage 1 closed impeller with OEM 13Cr martensitic stainless steel impeller (Part No. P204-IMP-01-SS).
- **Bearings:** Installed new paired angular contact thrust bearings (SKF 7318 BECBM) and cylindrical roller radial bearing (SKF NU 318 ECM). Radial clearance verified at 0.045 mm.
- **Shaft Sleeves & Seals:** Fitted new cartridge mechanical seal (John Crane Type 8648VRS) with tungsten carbide vs. premium carbon faces.
- **Suction Strainer:** Cleaned and pressure tested 40-mesh stainless steel basket strainer; installed high-differential pressure transmitter across strainer with alarm at 0.35 bar.

## 4. POST-OVERHAUL TESTING & OPERATING PARAMETERS
- Suction Pressure: **4.6 bar**
- Discharge Pressure: **68.2 bar** at rated flow of 185 m³/h.
- DE Bearing Temperature: **58.5°C** (steady state after 12 hours continuous run).
- NDE Bearing Temperature: **54.1°C**.
- Total vibration (overall RMS): **1.65 mm/s** (ISO 10816-3 Class II Zone A).

## 5. PREVENTATIVE RECOMMENDATIONS
- Implement daily delta-P logging across suction strainer S-204.
- Perform ultrasonic bearing acoustic monitoring every 14 days.
- Flush and clean API Plan 23 seal cooler heat exchangers every 6 months.
