# Heavy Industrial Valve & Flange Maintenance Standard Operating Procedure
**Document Ref:** SOP-MECH-VLV-402  
**Classification:** Mechanical Integrity Standard (Simulated Decision Support Manual)  
**Applicable Equipment:** API 600 Gate Valves, High-Pressure Globe Valves, ANSI Class 300/600 Flanges

---

## 1. Equipment Identification & Nomenclature
- **Tag Prefix MOV**: Motor-Operated Valve (e.g. `MOV-4102-B` - Crude Distillation Unit Atmospheric Column Bottoms Isolation).
- **Tag Prefix PRV**: Pressure Relief Valve (e.g. `PRV-108-A` - Secondary Header Safety Relief).
- **Tag Prefix VLV**: Manual Isolation Valve (e.g. `VLV-204-CrudeInlet`).

---

## 2. Visual Defect & Corrosion Classification

| Defect Category | Visual Symptoms & Identifiers | Severity Rating | Mandatory Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pitting Corrosion on Flange Ring** | Localized dark cavities, deep circular rust spots on sealing surface | **HIGH** | Immediate ultrasonic thickness measurement; replace spiral wound gasket; polish flange face if pit depth < 0.5mm. |
| **Atmospheric Flaking / Surface Rust** | Brown/orange reddish surface powder on bonnet or body exterior | **LOW** | Wire-brush clean, apply epoxy primer coat; regular bi-monthly inspection. |
| **Stem Packing Leakage / Crystalline Salt Cake** | White or yellow crust accumulation around packing gland bolts | **MEDIUM** | Tighten gland follower nuts uniformly to 45 Nm; if leak persists, schedule live-line repacking. |
| **Bolting Corrosion / Necking** | Stud bolt thread loss > 20% or diameter reduction | **CRITICAL** | Hot-bolting replacement protocol under permit-to-work; never torque corroded bolts while under operating pressure. |

---

## 3. Multimodal Diagnostic Decision Tree
When evaluating visual inspection imagery:
1. Identify equipment tag from nameplate or stamped flange rim (e.g. `MOV-4102-B`).
2. Detect visible corrosion type: Distinguish between benign surface scaling vs. severe flange interface pitting.
3. Check operating pressure rating: Class 300 (51.0 bar max @ 38°C) vs. Class 600 (102.1 bar max @ 38°C).
4. Flag any visual ambiguities or obscured tag characters clearly as *Tentative / Operator Verification Required*.
5. Generate structured Maintenance Recommendation with estimated urgency.
