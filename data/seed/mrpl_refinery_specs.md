# MRPL Refinery Hydrocarbon Quality Specification Benchmark Standard
**Document Ref:** MRPL-STD-QC-2026-V4  
**Classification:** Internal Operating Standard (Simulated Decision Support Benchmark)  
**Applicable Units:** Continuous Catalytic Reforming (CCR), Motor Spirit Quality Upgradation (MSQU), Diesel Hydro-Desulfurization (DHDS)

---

## 1. Scope & Quality Mandates
All finished hydrocarbon batches and intermediate blend components processed at the refinery must adhere strictly to Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) BS-VI / Euro-VI compliance limits before pipeline dispatch or tank farm transfer.

## 3. Compliance & Deviation Escalation Protocol
- **COMPLIANT**: Observed parameter is within the Regulatory Limit and Safe Tolerance.
- **DEVIATION (Level 1 Alert)**: Parameter exceeds Safe Tolerance but remains below Regulatory Ceiling. Requires batch blending adjustment.
- **CRITICAL DEVIATION (Level 2 Interlock)**: Parameter violates the Regulatory Upper Limit (e.g. Sulfur > 10.0 ppm or Benzene > 1.0% vol). Transfer pumps MUST be interlocked, and an Automated Compliance Report must be compiled and signed off by the Operations Shift Controller.
